The five SVG files in this directory (gb, nl, es, dk, fi) are taken from the
flag-icons package, version 7.5.0 — https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons — and
are redistributed here under its MIT licence, reproduced in full below.

They are vendored rather than installed: the language switcher needs exactly
these five files, they do not change, and serving them from this origin keeps
the control working without a request to anybody else's host. The only
modification is the removal of the package's own `id` attribute from each
root <svg> element; nothing referenced it.
