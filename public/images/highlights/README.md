# "What you might see" photographs

Every prebuilt itinerary in the custom-tour wizard lists the species and sights
you might see. Each of those already asks for its own photograph by name, and
the site renders whatever is here: a highlight with a photograph shows it as a
card in the itinerary dialog and as a thumbnail in the PDF and Word documents
the traveller downloads, and a highlight without one falls back to a plain
lettered tile. Nothing in the code or the content files needs changing when the
real images land — drop them in with these names and redeploy.

## Before you export

- JPEG, quality ~80, **1200 x 900** (4:3 landscape) is plenty. Next.js resizes
  on demand, and the same file is re-encoded down to ~700px inside the
  downloadable documents.
- Crop to the animal or the sight, not to the landscape it was in: these are
  shown at about 250px wide.
- Only publish photographs you have the right to use. A species photographed on
  one of Nandana's own walks is worth more here than a stock image, and the
  documents that carry them go out under the company's name.
- A photograph you do not have is better left out than substituted: a picture
  of the wrong bird under an endemic's name is the one mistake a birding
  client will never forgive.
